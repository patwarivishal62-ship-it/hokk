## 1. PAGE POSITIONING STATEMENT

The East's page is the storytelling chapter: this is the region where saris carry narratives (Baluchari's epic pallus), where the world's most honoured weaving technique lives (UNESCO-listed jamdani), where pattern hides in yarn (Sambalpuri bandha), where wild silk builds a city (Bhagalpur), and where old cloth is reborn as art (kantha). The page handles Bengal's muslin tragedy honestly — as history, not guilt — and positions the contemporary sari revival as the tradition's own next chapter.

## 4. THE FIVE TRADITIONS (shipped)

1. **Baluchari — West Bengal.** Pallu as sequential art; Subho Thakur's remembered role stated as local memory; GI 2012 (WB Patent Information Centre); jacquard assistance framed honestly.
2. **Jamdani — Bengal.** Kandi-stick technique, two-weaver looms, both-face motifs; UNESCO Representative List 2013; Bengal GI 2016 (per most sources — stated with softness); muslin lineage without romance-overdose.
3. **Sambalpuri Bandha — Odisha.** Tie-dye intelligence; single and double ikat; GI 2012 (Directorate of Textiles & Handloom, Odisha); Odia identity wear.
4. **Bhagalpuri Tussar — Bihar.** Forest-fibre economy; tens of thousands of households; GI 2013 (Bhagalpur Tussar Silk Development Samiti).
5. **Nakshi Kantha — Bengal.** Recycling as art; running stitch taken furthest; household→workshop history; no GI claim made (none verified).
