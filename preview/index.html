The Philosophy

Kala is the art. Katha is the story.

Every House of Kala Katha piece begins its life on a loom, in a village, in the hands of someone who has spent a lifetime perfecting a technique passed down through generations. We take that craft — the ikat, the kalamkari, the patola — and we re-cut it, re-imagine it, re-fit it for the life you actually live.

This isn't heritage you wear once a year. This is heritage that belongs in your everyday life.

The Story in the Cloth

The Craft Map of India

Four regions, hundreds of looms, thousands of hands. Hover over the map, click a region, and read its katha — the handloom cultures it carries and the speciality that makes it sing.

Craft Region

North India

The cold mountains and royal plains of the north — where wool meets gold.

5 handloom traditions · North India

Handloom cultures

  • Pashmina & Kani shawls — Kashmir
  • Kullu & Kinnauri shawls — Himachal
  • Phulkari embroidery — Punjab
  • Chikankari — Lucknow
  • Banarasi brocade — Varanasi

Speciality / USP

Luxe winter weaves and royal brocades. The north's story is fine wool, silk zari and the regal Banarasi loom.

Craft Region

West & Central India

The print-and-dye powerhouse of the country — from the deserts of Kutch to the looms of Paithan.

7 handloom traditions · West & Central India

Handloom cultures

  • Bandhani tie-dye — Gujarat & Rajasthan
  • Patola double ikat — Patan
  • Ajrakh block print — Kutch
  • Bagru & Sanganeri prints — Rajasthan
  • Paithani silk — Maharashtra
  • Chanderi & Maheshwari — Madhya Pradesh
  • Kosa silk — Chhattisgarh

Speciality / USP

The masters of resist-dye and block print. Home to the legendary Patola — one of the world's most complex weaves.

Craft Region

East India

Featherlight silks and narrative weaving, from the Ganges delta to the Northeast hills.

6 handloom traditions · East India

Handloom cultures

  • Ikat of Odisha — single ikat
  • Sambalpuri & Bomkai — Odisha
  • Jamdani — Bengal
  • Kantha embroidery — Bengal
  • Bhagalpuri tussar — Bihar
  • Muga & Eri silk — Assam

Speciality / USP

Air-light muslins and golden Muga silk. The east weaves stories into every thread.

Craft Region

South India

Temple-town silks and the double-ikat heartland, from Pochampally to Kanchipuram.

6 handloom traditions · South India

Handloom cultures

  • Pochampally & Puttapaka ikat — Telangana
  • Kanjeevaram silk — Tamil Nadu
  • Uppada jamdani — Andhra
  • Kalamkari art — Andhra
  • Mysore silk & Ilkal — Karnataka
  • Kasavu — Kerala

Speciality / USP

The seat of the double ikat. South India dresses temples and weddings in silk and gold zari.
