The Philosophy

Kala is the art. Katha is the story.

Every House of Kala Katha piece begins its life on a loom, in a village, in the hands of someone who has spent a lifetime perfecting a technique passed down through generations. We take that craft — the ikat, the kalamkari, the patola — and we re-cut it, re-imagine it, re-fit it for the life you actually live.

This isn't heritage you wear once a year. This is heritage that belongs in your everyday life.

The Story in the Cloth

This Season's Katha: The Ikat of Pochampally

Kala is the craft. Katha is everything around it — where it comes from, who makes it, why the pattern looks the way it does, and how it found its way onto a jacket you'd actually wear on a Tuesday.

Region

Pochampally, Telangana

A village whose name is now synonymous with the double-ikat weave.

Technique

Ikat — Resist-Dyed Yarn

Threads are tie-dyed before weaving, so the pattern lives inside the yarn itself.

Motif

The Diamond Chowka

A geometric motif once reserved for temple textiles, now a print for the everyday.

Reimagined as

Shirts, Co-ords & Jackets

Cut into relaxed silhouettes that move with your day, not against it.
